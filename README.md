# Fuel Analytics

An intelligent vehicle health and fuel management platform.

Fuel Analytics helps drivers monitor fuel consumption, track vehicle performance, and reduce operating costs through advanced analytics and AI-powered insights. By transforming everyday refueling records into actionable data, the application helps you make smarter driving and maintenance decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ⛽ Smart Fuel Tracking

Record every refueling with detailed information, including:

- Odometer reading
- Fuel quantity
- Fuel price
- Total cost
- Fuel type
- Driving conditions

Track your complete refueling history with accurate, real-world data.

---

### 🚗 Vehicle Health Monitoring

Monitor your vehicle's condition using intelligent performance indicators:

- Overall Vehicle Health Score
- Fuel Efficiency Score
- Engine Maintenance Score
- Driving Behavior Score
- Cost Management Score

Receive maintenance recommendations based on your driving habits and vehicle performance.

---

### 📊 Fuel Economy Analytics

Understand how efficiently your vehicle operates through:

- Average Fuel Economy
- Cost per Distance
- Consumption Trends
- Historical Performance
- Fuel Cost Analysis

Interactive charts make it easy to identify changes over time.

---

### 🧠 AI Technician Diagnostics

Every three new refueling records unlock a comprehensive AI-generated diagnostic report.

The system analyzes:

- Fuel efficiency trends
- Driving behavior
- Vehicle performance
- Potential maintenance issues
- Personalized recommendations

Designed to simulate professional vehicle diagnostics using real driving data.

---

### 🌬 Speed & Drag Simulator

Visualize how different driving conditions affect fuel consumption.

Experiment with:

- Vehicle speed
- Air conditioning usage
- Open windows
- Roof racks
- Aerodynamic drag

Learn how small driving habit changes can significantly improve fuel economy.

---

### 🌍 Global Unit System

Fuel Analytics supports multiple measurement systems:

- Metric (Kilometers / Liters)
- US Imperial (Miles / Gallons)
- UK Hybrid (Miles / Liters)

Units are automatically selected based on the user's region and can be changed at any time.
